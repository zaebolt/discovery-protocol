# Concept scoring (critic, blind)

Score **each concept on each dimension, 1–10**, with a one-line justification wherever the number isn't obvious. Use the full scale. **There is no passing score and you are not told how the scores will be used.** Measure honestly and rank strictly.

- **User value**: serves the prioritised needs.
- **Journey clarity**: the primary journey is concrete, short, and gets to the outcome without detours.
- **Workflow fit**: fits how these users already work and where the product lives; low adoption friction.
- **Must coverage (truthful)**: check each one-line must claim against the journey. Penalise claims the journey doesn't support.
- **Feasibility & risk**: buildable within the constraints; known UX, technical, and adoption risks.
- **Distinctness**: makes a bet the other concepts don't. If two concepts can't be told apart by what they can do, say so.

Rules: produce a strict ranking with no ties. If two overall scores are within 0.2, differentiate them or explain why they are genuinely equivalent. Name the **kill** (eliminate first, and why) and the **bet** (the one you'd back with your own time, and why). For each concept, name the unconfirmed A-ID it collapses without.

Write `artifacts/concepts/concept-scores-vN.md`: score table with overall mean (one decimal), forced ranking, kill + bet, per-concept note, and **which 2 to sketch** and why. The 2 to sketch are usually the top 2. Choose differently if a lower-ranked concept is more uncertain and sketching it would resolve more.
